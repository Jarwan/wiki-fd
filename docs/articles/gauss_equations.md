### Non circular, non equatorial orbits
$e \neq 0$ and $i \neq 0$

In RSW frame [RSW frame](local_orbital_frames.md) :

$$
\frac{da}{dt} = \frac{2}{n\sqrt{1 - e^2}} \{ e \sin{\nu} \vec{R} + \frac{p}{r} \vec{S} \}
$$

$$
\frac{de}{dt} = \frac{\sqrt{1 - e^2}}{na} \{ \sin{\nu} \vec{R} + (\cos{\nu} + \frac{e + \cos{\nu}}{1 + e\cos{\nu}}) \vec{S} \}
$$


where :
  * $a$ semi-major axis ($m$)
  * $n$ mean motion / orbital angular speed ($rad.s^{-1}$)
  * $e$ eccentricity (no dimension)
  * $\nu$ true anomaly ($rad$)
  * $p$ semi-parameter ($m$)
  * $r$ radius ($m$)
  * $(\vec{R}, \vec{S}, \vec{W})$ unit vectors of the RSW frame
